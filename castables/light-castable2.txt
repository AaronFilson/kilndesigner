Research on the combination of Dawn dish soap and Xanthan Gum with bentonite is less documented than their use in Portland cement, but individual effects on clay show significant potential for stabilizing and thickening mixtures.

Interaction with Bentonite

While NighthawkInLight's specific formula focuses on aerating cement, its components interact with bentonite in the following ways:
• Xanthan Gum (XG): Xanthan gum is a powerful stabilizer for bentonite. It creates a gel-like structure that fills pore gaps between clay particles, significantly increasing shear strength, cohesion, and viscosity. It also helps bentonite maintain stability in salt-contaminated water, which typically causes clay to clump or fail.
• Dawn Dish Soap: In cement, soap acts as a foaming agent to create micro-air bubbles. In a bentonite slurry, surfactants like those in Dawn can alter surface tension, potentially aiding in the dispersion of clay particles or creating an "air-crete" style foam if whipped.

Potential Effects of the Combo

Combining these could theoretically produce a lightweight, stabilized clay-foam or a highly viscous "slip" with unique properties:
• Enhanced Workability: Xanthan gum provides "body" and prevents the foam from collapsing quickly, while the soap generates the air pockets.
• Increased Water Retention: Both additives are known to increase the moisture-holding capacity of clay, which can be useful for liners or ecological projects.
• Reduced Density: Similar to "aerated concrete," this mix could result in a significantly lighter bentonite-based material.
