## 2. Science review fixes

Each fix follows the engine rules: a source in `docs/research/` or a data sheet, working steps
for every new number, and a refusal with a reason outside the checked range (ADR 0009). Each
gets tests first, then the golden reports are regenerated and the diffs read line by line.

### Fix 1: a door or car in a wall the roof stands on (critical)

- In `model.py`, next to the catenary's side-wall check (`model.py:477-493`), refuse:
  - a sprung arch with its door or car in a springing wall (left or right);
  - a sail dome with its door in any wall it bears on, unless the design has a lintel or arch
    over the opening that carries the roof. Until the research says how to size one, refuse.
- Move the five sprung-arch examples' doors and cars to the front or back wall, and check each
  still builds. The car kiln's car rolls out of an end wall.
- The user's PNW design (dome, front door) will be refused. Tell the user before it ships, and
  say what to change.
- Research gap: how a door arch or lintel under a sprung roof is sized. Note it in
  `docs/research/` as open, not invented.

### Fix 2: organics burning off during dry-out (critical)

- In the dry-out check, add up the heat from every mix in the lining (only layers that reach
  the burnout temperature, which also fixes the review's "organics counted in a layer that never
  reaches 240 °C" bug), and compare the total rate with what the schedule's step needs, not with
  the burners' installed power.
- Leave out mixes the design doesn't use (the "unused mixes" bug).
- Test: `diy-castable-test-kiln` must report the burnout step (about 122 kW against 3–25 kW).
- Depends on `castables.md` and `castables-minerals.md` for the organics' heat of combustion.
  If those files can't be found, cite the data sheets directly or leave the check out with a
  stated reason.

### Fix 3: an oversized breaker (major, safety)

- In `electric.py`, check the breaker against NEC 422.11(E) as `electric-and-thermal.md` §3
  records it: refuse (or flag as an error) a breaker above the rule's limit for the kiln's
  current.
- Fix the electric example's 60 A breaker on a 4 kW kiln to a size the rule allows.
- While in `electric.py`: fix the hot resistivity label at `electric.py:291` (Ω·m shown as
  Ω·mm²/m); the strict xfail test then passes and its marker comes off.

### Fix 4: gas supply and pressure in the cold (major)

- Tank table: replace the "⅓ full" figures, which are the 50%-full rule, with the source's ⅓
  figures, or derate them with a cited factor.
- 100 lb cylinders: read the table by liquid temperature, and take the liquid as colder than the
  air under draw (the review's 20 °F), with that drop cited.
- Tank pressure ceiling: same liquid cooling.
- The 10 psi cylinder limit: leave room for the regulator's droop, from the regulator's data
  sheet.
- Test: a cold-day design that passes today must now be flagged as stalling.
- Needs the supply tables' source in `docs/research/`; check which file they come from before
  changing numbers.

### Fix 5: burners listed "at 5.0 psi" below their rating

- In `gas.py`'s `_burner_options`, a burner whose rating at 5 psi is above the load runs at the
  pressure that gives the load, read from its curve; don't report the load at 5 psi.
- Feed the burner's real draw into the supply check, so the 1.7–2.2× under-count goes away.
- While in `gas.py`: report a finding when no burner fits (`gas.py:451`).
