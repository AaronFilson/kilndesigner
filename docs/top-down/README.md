# Top-down gas firing

Research and design work on firing a gas kiln from the top: burners in the lid, or at the top of the side wall. This is work in progress, last updated 2026-10-27. Nothing here is engineering data for the engine yet.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `design-notes.md` | Start here: the kiln, what was found, three candidate designs, safety, open questions |
| `research-report.md` | Cited research report: precedents, burner hardware, engineering and safety issues |
| `research-notes/` | The four research notes the report was written from (pottery and art kilns, forges and industrial furnaces, burner hardware and patents, engineering and safety) |
| `models/` | First-principles scripts behind the design numbers. Run with `python3 docs/top-down/models/<script>.py` |

The research notes read most forum pages through search-engine excerpts, because those sites blocked direct access. Product photos were not viewed. Each note says how its sources were read.
