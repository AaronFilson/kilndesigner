Branch `plan-review` after `ddcb1f5` (the HWI handbook's second reading merged). The owner asked
for this part to work well before the plan goes on. Scope:
- engine v2's statics and heat and their tests;
- the reference dataset (`core/tests/v2/reference_kilns.yaml`);
- the owner's answers of 2026-11-17 (`docs/planning/11-17-26-inputs.md`);

from v2 and isn't repeated here.

## Summary

- **Statics is sound.**
  - 52 kilns stand cold and hot, and N1–N6 all fail in the numbers.
  - Tobe stands hot, worked back from standing.
  - Its worked-back numbers were recorded loosely; fixed (§3.4).
- **Heat passes its suite, but most of the owner's 11-17 answers aren't applied.** The
  test-side code still carries four rules the owner overturned:
  - Rule 6 as a ⅕–¼ band;
  - stoke holes as air intakes;
  - the fire mouth as two corners;
  - Kitade as one chamber.
  The bar's figures stand on those rules until the answers are applied and the bar rerun.
- **Four faults came in during this round.**
  - Tobe's setting bands went stale when its walls changed. The full suite's one failure was
    this (§3.1).
  - Suzuki's total compared seven chambers' wood against three chambers' figure (§3.2).
  - Hollyweird's new side-door stoke hole became an air intake (§3.3).
  - Tobe's ring frontier was run on the old walls (§3.4).
  All but Hollyweird's are fixed. Hollyweird's needs the stoke-hole answer applied in the engine.
- **The docs had drifted.** Many still called answered questions open. Test docstrings and xfail
  reasons stated what the tests no longer do. Fixed (§3.5, §3.6).
- **So the plan is reordered** (`02-plan.md`, "Next, in order"). Item 1 is now "finish this
  part": apply the answers, then rerun the bar. Only after that does item 2 fold the test-side
  code into the engine, so the engine doesn't take in overturned rules.

## 3. Faults and drift, and what was fixed

### 3.1 Tobe's setting bands (the suite's one failure)

Tobe's walls went from 9½ to 13½ in (`c99a14f`). The seven `setting` facts are the method's
bands on the chamber zones as v2 traces them, and they weren't regenerated. v2 traces the zones
0.1–0.2 % larger on the new walls:
- chambers 1 and 7: 63.327 → 63.393 m³;
- chambers 2–6: 63.459 → 63.590 m³.

The slow geometry test caught it. `c99a14f` went in without that test, and it should have run.

**Fixed:** the seven facts are regenerated from the method (`scratch/review/tobe_setting.py`):
[4150, 27898] kg at the ends and [4161, 27949] in the middle.

### 3.2 Suzuki's total wood

`14fe60d` held chambers 4–7 to their printed 5 h, so the held run now goes on past chamber 3.
`row_cases.held_results` sums every side stage, so its total became chambers 1–7's. The test
still compared it with chambers 1–3's 450 bundles. It passed, but it compared the wrong
quantities.

**Fixed:**
- The test is now `test_suzuki_burns_its_bundles_in_chambers_1_to_7`, against the derived 930
  bundles.
- The dataset's gap for chambers 4–7's bundles is removed.
- `row_cases.py`'s note of 4.38 t for chambers 1–3 is dated to before the change.

### 3.3 Hollyweird's side door as an air intake

`ff8d467` added the side-stoked firebox's stoke hole, 9 × 13½ in. Where the dataset gives no
intake, the engine takes a design's stoke hole as a fully open intake (`paths.intake_els`). So
Hollyweird's back stage now draws its air through the side door. That is against the owner's
answer (§3): stoke holes are covered after each stoking. The stage's air should be the gas from
upstream plus the 5 in tube under the second grate (p.116).

**Not fixed here.** The fix is the stoke-hole answer applied in the engine, which is the plan's
item 1. Recorded in the inputs (§3) and in `hollyweird.md`.

### 3.4 Tobe's worked-back numbers

Three faults:
- **The wrong walls.** The ring frontier was run on the old 9½ in walls (9.70–9.84 in).
- **The wrong status.** It was recorded as an `inferred` fact. Decision 17 as amended infers a
  property of the kiln's own material, and a ring's thickness is a geometry.
- **Loose strengths.** The two inferred strengths, 19.5 and 30.5 psi, were the middles of the
  test's 1 psi brackets.

**Fixed.**
- A finer bisection on the design's 13½ in walls (`scratch/tobe/worked_back.py`):

  | Ring | No admissible state | Admissible |
  |---|---|---|
  | 13½ in | 19.50 psi | 19.53 psi |
  | 9 in | 30.50 psi | 30.53 psi |
  | on 25 psi | 9.74 in | 9.77 in |

- The frontier is still about 9¾ in. It now sits in the assumed ring's reason, with the walls
  named.
- The two strengths stand, and their reasons carry the bisection.
- `test_tobe_is_worked_back_from_standing` pins them to ±0.1 psi (19.4/19.6 and 30.4/30.6) where
  it pinned ±0.5.
- Updated: `tobe.md` and the inputs (§12, with a dated correction).
