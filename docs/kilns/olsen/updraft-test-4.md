# Updraft test kiln 4, 4 × 4 × 5 ft stacking (`updraft-test-4`)

Example: [`examples/olsen/updraft-test-4.yaml`](../../../examples/olsen/updraft-test-4.yaml).
Research notes: `docs/research/olsen-kilns.md`, `updraft-test-1` to `updraft-test-4`.

Test kiln 3 with its width and length raised to 4 ft: it "evened out perfectly". Olsen's
conclusion: with floor burners, equal height and width matter most.

## Book pages

Olsen, *The Kiln Book*, 4th ed., p.74: text only. No figure.

## What's modelled

57 × 57 in (the stacking plus 4½ in each side), 60 in to the springline, a No. 1 arch; eighteen
MR-750 floor burners in 2½ in ports on natural gas at 8 in w.c.; 7 in IFB walls; a hinged door;
cone 10.
