# Fast-fire kiln, Anderson Ranch, 1977

Design file: `examples/olsen/fast-fire-anderson.yaml`. An 18 ft³ Olsen fast-fire wood downdraft
built at a summer workshop in Aspen, Colorado (8600 ft).

## Sources

Olsen, *The Kiln Book*, 4th ed.: p.141 (Fig 5-41, photo, bricked-up door; text), p.142 (Table 5-2,
first firing), p.82 (Aspen's elevation). The design itself is the `fast-fire` kiln
(`docs/kilns/olsen/fast-fire.md`). Notes: `docs/research/olsen-kilns.md`.

## Feature by feature

| Feature | Book | Model | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Size | "18 cu. ft" (Fig 5-41) | 27 × 30 × 36 in + arch, 17.9 ft³ | depth chosen to give 18 ft³ |
| Door | bricked up (Fig 5-41, p.141) | whole front, bricked | matches |
| Everything else | not given; the fast-fire design | fast-fire's sizes | assumed |
| Altitude | Aspen 8600 ft | elevation 8600 ft | the book doesn't say whether the high-altitude sizes (6th firebox course, 13½" flue and chimney) were used |
| Spy holes | the photo shows a numbered cone/brick layout on the door, no holes readable | engine default | not given |

## Results (build of 2026-11-08)

17.9 ft³; 2345 °F; 7.97 kW at the top; $6,511 priced items; W1 (K-23 above its rating), as
`fast-fire`. Table 5-2: cone 10 down everywhere in about 10 h.
