# Olsen's 36 ft³ dense-brick cross-draft (`olsen-crossdraft-36`)

The kiln Olsen fired on six 36,000 BTU/hr venturi burners in 12–15 h, his evidence that most gas
kilns are overpowered. Design file: `examples/olsen/olsen-crossdraft-36.yaml`.

Olsen, *The Kiln Book*, 4th ed.: p.226 (text only). No figure.
